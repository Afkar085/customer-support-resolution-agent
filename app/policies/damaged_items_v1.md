## [section_1] Reporting a damaged item

Damage must be reported within 30 days of delivery. Before any resolution is
prepared, the agent must collect a short description of the damage and at least
one photograph showing the item and its outer packaging. Without both, the case
is incomplete and the agent must ask the customer for the missing item rather
than guess.

## [section_2] Resolution options

Once a damaged-item report is complete, the available resolutions are a
replacement of the same model, or a return followed by a refund under the
Refund Eligibility Policy. A refund for a damaged item still requires the item
to be returned, received and inspected, unless section 3 applies.

## [section_3] Waived return for low-value and hazardous items

Where the item is hazardous to ship -- swollen batteries, cracked lithium cells
-- the return requirement is waived and the case must be referred to a support
reviewer, who decides the resolution. An agent must not decide a waiver.

## [section_4] Damage reported outside the window

A damage report made more than 30 days after delivery falls outside this policy.
The customer should be directed to the manufacturer warranty. No refund is
prepared under this policy.
