## [section_1] Return window

Returns may be started within 15 days of delivery.

## [section_2] Refunds for returned items

A refund may be issued once the return has been received at the warehouse.
Inspection is not required for orders below 2,000 rupees.

## [section_3] Approval requirement

Refunds below 1,000 rupees may be issued by an agent without reviewer approval.
