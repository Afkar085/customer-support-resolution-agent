All amounts in this policy are in Indian rupees and apply to the single
Voltline Electronics storefront.

## [section_1] Scope and definitions

This policy covers refunds to the original payment method for orders placed on
the Voltline Electronics store. A *refund* means returning captured funds for
an order. A *return* means the physical movement of goods back to the Voltline
warehouse. The two are tracked separately: a return can exist without a refund,
and a refund is never issued on the strength of a return request alone.

## [section_2] Return window

A customer may start a return within 30 days of the delivery date recorded
against the order. A return started after that window is outside policy and
cannot be refunded under this section. Where no delivery date is recorded, the
window has not started and the order must be treated as not yet delivered.

## [section_3] Refunds for returned items

Refunds for returned items become eligible when both of the following are
recorded against the order:

1. the return is marked as received at the warehouse, and
2. inspection of the returned item has passed.

Until both conditions hold, the correct answer to a refund request is that the
refund is not yet eligible, together with the condition that is outstanding. A
return that has been received but not yet inspected is *pending*, not refused.

## [section_4] Refund amount

The refundable amount is the amount captured for the order, less any amount
already refunded and less any amount reserved by a refund that is in progress.
A refund may never exceed that balance. Partial refunds below the item price
are out of scope for this policy version and require review.

## [section_5] Approval requirement

A support reviewer must approve every refund before it is issued, regardless of
amount, customer tier or how clear the case appears. An approval applies to one
specific order, amount and currency. If any of those change, the earlier
approval no longer applies and a new approval must be obtained.

## [section_6] Refund timelines

Once issued, a refund reaches the customer's original payment method within 5
to 7 working days. Until the payment provider confirms settlement, the correct
description of the state is that the refund has been submitted and is
processing -- not that it is complete.
